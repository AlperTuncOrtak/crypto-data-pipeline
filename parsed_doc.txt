Claude analyzes what you show it — it can&#39;t watch 40 live charts. The edge is having ONE consistent analyst apply your rules the same way every time, not infinite coverage. 🧠 ANALYZE — Chart Analysis Workflow The 4-step read — run every chart through this (prompt #6 automates it): Step What Claude looks at 1. Structure Trend direction, higher-highs/lower-lows, the bigger picture 2. Levels Support, resistance, recent swing points 3. Setup match Does this match one of MY written setups — or none? 4. Invalidation Where would this idea be proven wrong? Honest limit: Claude reads a static image — it can be wrong about exact prices or fine detail. Treat its read as a second opinion that must agree with what YOU see, never as ground truth. Always confirm levels on the actual chart. 📐 Breakout, Support, Resistance &amp; Trend Prompts Breakout: &quot;Here&#39;s a chart of [ticker]: [screenshot]. Are they aligned or conflicting? What&#39;s the strongest bear case and the 3 ways it fails?&quot; Non-negotiables to keep in CLAUDE.md: max 1% risk per trade, stop at invalidation only (never widened), min 1:2 R:R, max 3 open positions, paper trade any new setup 30+ times first. 🔔 MONITOR — Alert Workflows Setting the alert (in TradingView): ☐ Right-click your level → Add Alert → set the condition ☐ Under Notifications, enable Webhook URL (requires a paid TV plan) and paste your endpoint ☐ In the alert message box, write JSON Claude can parse, e.g.: {&quot;ticker&quot;:&quot;{{ticker}}&quot;,&quot;price&quot;:&quot;{{close}}&quot;,&quot;setup&quot;:&quot;breakout&quot;} What receives it: a no-code tool like n8n or Make.com catches the webhook, logs it to alerts-log.md, and can forward it to Claude for an instant re-check against your rules (prompt #16). Then it pings your phone. You still place every trade manually — the webhook alerts and analyzes; it doesn&#39;t execute. 📊 Trade Review Workflow After every trade, screenshot the chart with your entry/exit marked and run prompt #18. Log to journal/: setup, result in R, did-I-follow-the-plan, one lesson. Weekly, Claude reads the journal and finds your patterns (prompt #19).
